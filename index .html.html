Community rules

One floor. Everything above it is your call.

🛑 The one hard rule

Illegal content, credible threats, doxxing, and harassment that crosses the line are never allowed, in any room. This is the only thing that isn't up for debate.

⚖️ How enforcement works

Everything else is loose by default — blunt opinions, dark humor, disagreement, and edgy takes are all fine. For threats and harassment specifically, violations escalate if they repeat within a rolling 2-week window:

1 Warning
2 1 week suspension
3 1 month suspension
4 Permanent restriction
